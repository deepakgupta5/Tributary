# Bounded source inspection

Inspected 2026-11-03 America/Vancouver (2026-11-04 UTC). Scope: the nine candidate publication families in the owner-approved proposal. This is an inspection record, not source admission or a live evidence run. The intended project use is **noncommercial research** with copying and retention of selected material. “Unresolved” means this inspection did not establish the relevant permission; it does not assert that use is prohibited. An inspectable webpage alone establishes no right to retain its text. No source documents or extracted nonprofit evidence are stored in this repository.

**Use labels:** `yes` means a specific inspected source statement supports the use, subject to its stated conditions; `conditional` means an item-specific check remains; `unresolved` means no basis was established; `restricted` means inspected terms expressly limit the proposed use; `failed` means access could not be confirmed. These are inspection classifications, not legal conclusions. “Factual notes and links” means human-authored facts/metadata with attribution, without copied passages. Text and field retention are separate.

## Relationships and trial use

The South Seattle Emerald story provides an **unverified discovery link** between a local report, named groups and a stated city fund. It does not establish their identities, funding status or suitability. The CRA and IRS datasets are separate official identity/filing references; an organization publication can add first-party context but is not independent confirmation of its own claims. Food Lifeline's own financial index links its reports and 990 documents; the IRS copy, if available, is the preferable separately governed filing endpoint. Vancouver Foundation's grant search and Seattle Foundation's publications may reveal routes, but neither was inspected down to a grant relationship. No organization or source was admitted.

The finite set tests BC/Washington official records, local reporting, organization publication and a potential funder route without forcing blocked material into evidence. A later trial needs source material sufficient to let QC and the fact-checker compare numbers, quotes, scope and qualifications: URL, publisher, document title, dates, locator, relevant table/field labels, and *permitted* short passages with adjacent qualifiers where required. A hash and a paraphrase alone cannot recheck an exact quote. Whole live articles, audio and transcripts remain outside permanent storage. Where copied context cannot be retained, the claim stays pending or is limited to factual notes that can be checked from the source during an authorized session.

## Inspection limits

This was browser/search inspection of entries, policy pages, indexes and selected representative pages. It did not test authenticated access, a JavaScript browser workflow for the CRA/IRS search applications, download CSV contents, inspect grant rows, or evaluate all article copyright notices. A 403 or timeout in the inspection tool is a tool-observed access result, not proof the publisher has barred all users. Search-result metadata identified some document URLs; those URLs are marked above when their contents or licence were not directly inspected. There was no admission, ingestion, live four-checkpoint review or source text committed to GitHub.
