# BC outbound trigger screen

Purpose: surface an evidence-backed hypothesis about an internal decision before an organization publicly asks for help. Do not require an admission of difficulty to create a discovery lead.

## Population gate
Start with the 754 historical revenue/staff-proxy matches. In parallel retain the 414 revenue matches with incomplete staffing for enrichment. A 2024 filing establishes historical fit only. Resolve the operating organization, service scope and latest available size before outreach preparation. Preserve near-band and unknown records separately.

Population exclusions (D062, 2026-11-06): hospitals, universities, religious organizations, and advocacy groups are excluded as buyers for the BC pilot; hospital foundations are separate entities and remain eligible. Qualification order is size fit → donor-base gate → trigger: from the T3010 revenue mix, tax-receipted gifts and fundraising revenue must be a material share of revenue; an organization funded predominantly by government contracts or fees goes to Monitor regardless of size fit (see PRD, "Current outbound target: mid-sized BC nonprofits").

## Three rules
| Trigger | Anchor | Corroborating clue on the same initiative | Decision question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supporter expansion | An identifiable existing supporter signals interest in a specific next phase, renewal or expansion | Upcoming proposal/board decision, conditional match, request for costed options, or impact reporting tied to release of funds | What proposal, cost estimate or evidence must be ready for whose decision, and when? |
| Funded delivery bottleneck | A named funded program or partnership has an identifiable obligation and milestone | Revised start date, extension, unfilled delivery role, missing approval, postponed rollout or partner handoff tied to that milestone | Which dependency is holding up the funded commitment and who owns it? |
| Leadership or growth risk | Confirmed ED leave/transition or material expansion | A concurrent launch, renewal, reporting deadline, service expansion or interim coverage issue with an identifiable delivery dependency | What work needs ownership during the transition and what happens if it slips? |

## Evidence procedure
1. Batch-match announcements, funder minutes, municipal decisions, annual reports, partner updates, job notices and procurement to known account IDs. Probe account-specific sources where an anchor appears.
2. Group facts by account AND initiative. Capture source, passage locator, publication/event date and milestone date separately. Reprints count once. One original can establish both facts when explicit.
3. Seek the strongest alternative explanation and the latest update: completed project, filled role, normal timeline, secured funding, or responsibility already assigned. A grant alone, an ED vacancy alone or a generic appeal stays a watch clue. Evidence about a different project cannot corroborate the anchor.
4. No usable evidence: unknown/monitor. Anchor alone: watch for a second clue. Anchor plus related clue plus a still-open decision window: inferred live trigger, eligible for discovery preparation after four checks. Explicit evidence of the internal bottleneck or owner-authorized confirmation: confirmed trigger. A resolved or contradicted hypothesis returns to monitor. Silence cannot confirm or disprove need.
5. Produce one card: facts with dates and sources; internal hypothesis labelled as inference; why now; counterevidence; one neutral discovery question. Keep funding availability, procurement authority and willingness unknown unless documented. Run collection/QC, researcher, skeptic and fact-checker as separate executions before surfacing the card. Outreach stays manual.
6. For efficiency scan shared sources across the whole pool, rotate account-specific checks on a bounded batch, and record last check, sources attempted and access gaps. Test yield on an initial batch before extending individual searches to all 754. Use owner feedback to measure whether the hypothesis was worth a conversation.
